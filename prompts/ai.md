# Section brief: AI & Society

Editor: Lester. In force from 9 November 2026. One link an issue, tied to the story arc "AI and society".

Scope: what AI changes in people's lives: work and careers, study and exams, art and creativity, relationships, trust and truth, power and who controls the tools. These readers are at the start of the AI era, as Gen X was at the start of the internet. The aim is that they understand its social and cultural impact without feeling they must chase every new tool.

## Ranking

1. Highest: essays and reported pieces on how AI changes something human (work, learning, art, relationships), especially in India or for young people. Example of the right kind: The Deep View, "AI can copy the song, not the reason we sing".
2. Then: what AI gets wrong, and who is accountable (errors, bias, deepfakes, job losses, regulation).
3. Lower, and usually left out: product launches, model releases, benchmarks, funding rounds, company gossip and "10 tools you must try".

## Sources

Any source in the list may supply this section. A `detect` source may be linked here only for a plain-fact story (a law passed, a study published), never for a political argument.

## Tone

Plain and curious, never hype. No jargon a 19-year-old would have to look up; if a term is needed, the headline gives its sense.
