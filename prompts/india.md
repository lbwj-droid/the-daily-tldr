# Section brief: India

Editor: Lester. In force from 27 October 2026 until he changes it. This replaces the Campus Watch focus, whose priorities now steer the whole India section.

Scope: general India news, judged by the general editorial rules. The ranking below decides which stories win the two places.

Background for recognising stories (as reported by Reuters and AP in October 2026; never put any of this in a headline unless the item itself says it): a student- and youth-led movement is demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar over the electoral-roll revision (SIR) and alleged vote-rigging ("vote chori"). It builds on earlier 2026 student protests over NEET exam paper leaks and the National Education Policy (NEP). Visible faces include Neha Bora (AISA national president); the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), whose spokespersons are Abhijeet Dipke, Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka, and whose schools campaign is "School Thik Karo" (Fix Our Schools); the INC and Rahul Gandhi, whose education campaign is "Chhatron Ki Goonj"; and Vishal Dadlani. "CJP" always means the Cockroach Janta Party.

## Ranking

1. Highest: items that make a story's stakes and demands clear: explainers, and interviews with the people driving it (for the student movement: Neha Bora; CJP spokespersons Abhijeet Dipke, Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka; Rahul Gandhi and the INC; Vishal Dadlani; Yogendra Yadav; Prashant Bhushan).
2. Then: new facts on accountability stories: the Election Commission and the SIR, exams and education, courts, police action, and the government's response. When the Election Commission or a ministry has issued its own statement, prefer an item that quotes or links it.
3. Lower: items that only show clashes, crowd sizes or reactions.

The student movement is the running story of this period, but it does not get the section by default: pick it when there is a new development, and give the place to a bigger India story when there is one.

## Sources

Prefer, in this order: interviews with Neha Bora on any channel, and aisa.in; interviews with CJP spokespersons on any channel; INC, including Rahul Gandhi interviews; Dhruv Rathee; Griha Atul; Mojo Story; Naman Shrivastava; interviews with Yogendra Yadav or Prashant Bhushan on any channel. The general rule stands: never link a `detect` source for a political story.

## Tone

The snarky voice may punch up at authorities. Never mock protesters, detainees or anyone injured. An allegation stays an allegation in the headline.
