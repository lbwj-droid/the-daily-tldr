# Focus brief: mideast (draft, not yet written)

Copy this file, fill in each part in plain English, and point a focus in config/focus.json at it.
Where this brief sets a rule that differs from prompts/editorial.md, the brief wins for this section only.
The "Leave out" rules in editorial.md always apply.

## 1. Scope
What counts. Name the specific events, places, institutions and issues. List the boundary cases that are in and the ones that are out.

## 2. Contrast and context
Which related stories elsewhere to include for comparison, and what is being compared (tactics, state response, outcomes, cost).

## 3. Angle
One sentence: what the readers should understand by the end of the focus period. Claude ranks stories by how much they move readers towards this.

## 4. Headline tone
Snarky Drudge voice, straight news voice, or a mix (say when each applies).

## 5. Sources
Outlets, channels and journalists to prefer for this topic (add their feeds under "sources" in config/focus.json), and any to avoid.

## 6. Mix
For example: up to 3 India links and 2 global links a day. Say what to do on quiet days.

## 7. Pinned starter links
Add these in the Sheet (section = the focus key, status = pin). List them here too, for the record.
